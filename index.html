Instructions

In this task, you will move a dot toward a green square using the arrow keys. On each trial, a green square appears on one side of the box. Press the arrow key that points toward it, and the dot will move.

Before each trial, you will make a prediction: will the dot actually go to the green square when you press the key? You will get most points from correct prediction, and the least points from incorrect prediction.

  • Press Y for Yes: I predict that the dot will go to the green square.
    • Correct prediction earns 10 points. Incorrect prediction earns 0 points.
  • Press N for No: I predict that the dot will not go to the green square.
    • Correct prediction earns 10 points. Incorrect prediction earns 0 points.
  • Press U for Unsure: I am not sure.
    • Earns 5 points.

Your total score is shown in the top-left corner. After every 10 trials, you will be asked to rate how much control you felt.

The goal is to earn as many points as you can.
